# Public footprint research: what the web says about Reef Support

Internal research note for the owner, compiled on 19 November 2026 by a web search pass. Nothing in this file goes on the site. Its purpose is to show which `CONTENT.md` items independent sources support, which open items in `CLAUDE.md` section 6 they could close, where older coverage disagrees with `CONTENT.md`, and which old claims are still circulating and must not be repeated.

## How to read this note

- **Method.** All findings come from web search result snippets and summaries. Direct page fetches were blocked in this session for every host tried (see "Could not reach" below), so no page was read in full. Treat every date, quote and figure here as "seen in a search index", to be checked by opening the link before it is used anywhere.
- **Tags.**
  - `CONFIRMS`: supports an item already in `CONTENT.md`.
  - `FILLS OPEN ITEM`: could close an item in `CLAUDE.md` section 6, once the owner checks it.
  - `NEW`: a fact not in `CONTENT.md`. It stays off the site until the owner adds it to `CONTENT.md`.
  - `CONTRADICTS`: differs from `CONTENT.md` and needs a decision.
  - `MUST NOT REPEAT`: older framing or claims banned by `CLAUDE.md` section 1. Linking to the source is allowed where `CONTENT.md` already lists it; quoting or paraphrasing the claim is not.
- **Removed names.** This file names banned products and programmes so the owner can find and clean them. The grep check in `CLAUDE.md` section 5 covers `src` and `public` only; never copy text from this file into either.

## Contradictions needing a decision

- **C1. "Superpixels to the Rescue" is not a paper.** It appears to be a section of Yohan Runhaar's University of Amsterdam project study, "A Study of Segmentation Methods for Corals in Benthic Photographs". `CONTENT.md` lists it as a 2022 paper, and Yohan's bio says the research "produced" it. Suggested fix for the owner: list the study under its full title as a student research report, with the reef.support summary or a PDF as the link, and drop the word "paper".
- **C2. Falling Walls Emerging Talent 2023.** The Falling Walls 2023 summit materials name Joseph Bedard, Yizhou Yu and Emma-Anne Karlsen as the Emerging Talents laureates. Kempers' profile confirms 2021 only in the snippets seen. If the 2023 item means he was selected into the Emerging Talent call rather than named a laureate, `CONTENT.md` should say so, or the item should be dropped until there is a letter or link.
- **C3. NOS item type.** `CONTENT.md` lists a 2020 NOS "Broadcast, Interview on the Copernicus Masters prize". The source found is a written NOS article of 9 December 2020 quoting Runhaar. It may include a video clip; the owner should check whether a separate TV or radio interview exists.
- **C4. ClimateLaunchpad.** `CONTENT.md` and the timeline say "ClimateLaunchpad global finalist / global finals". The ClimateLaunchpad page found says "National First prize winner" (2021). National winners usually go on to the global stage, but no source seen confirms the global final. Safer wording, if the owner agrees: "ClimateLaunchpad Netherlands national winner, 2021".
- **C5. Legal form.** A TU Delft page calls Reef Support a "non-profit social enterprise". The company is Reef Support B.V. This old wording should not be repeated, and it touches the open item on whether a foundation or NGO branch exists.
- **C6. Coral Lab minor.** `CONTENT.md` says the four students came from the "Delft Centre for Entrepreneurship minor". Delta says the TPM minor in international entrepreneurship and development. The owner should pick the correct name.
- **C7. Zindi 2021 date.** `CONTENT.md` dates the Ocean Hackathon Cape Town and Coral Classification Challenge to November 2021. The Zindi page snippet shows start and close dates in late October 2021. Ocean Innovation Africa itself may have run in November; the owner should check.
- **C8. Lombok location.** `CONTENT.md` says Kecinan Bay, West Lombok. One summary said North Lombok. Kecinan Bay sits near the West and North Lombok boundary; IBF should confirm the regency.
