Why reefs need support

Coral reefs cover less than 1% of the ocean floor but support about a quarter of all marine species. Warming seas, pollution and destructive fishing are pushing many reefs past their ability to recover on their own.

ReefSupport exists to close that gap: practical restoration on the water, and long-term care led by the people who live alongside the reef.

  • 25%of marine species depend on reefs
  • 1 billionpeople benefit from reef ecosystems
  • 50%of the world's coral lost since the 1950s

Our work

🪸

Coral restoration

We grow resilient coral fragments in nurseries and replant them on degraded reef sites.

🔬

Reef monitoring

Regular surveys track coral cover, bleaching and fish populations so we know what's working.

🤝

Community programmes

We train local divers and fishers as reef stewards and support sustainable livelihoods.

📚

Education

School visits, workshops and open data help more people understand and protect reefs.
